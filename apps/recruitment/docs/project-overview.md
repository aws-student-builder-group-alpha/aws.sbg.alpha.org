## Goals

- Help students find an eligible opportunity, submit one application and follow its status.
- Give one assigned evaluator a private recommendation workflow and administrators a clear publication workflow.
- Enforce identity, ownership, permissions, immutable submissions, safe retries and capacity on the server.
- Use free service tiers, subject to eligibility and measured capacity. The target is 1,000 applications, 100 concurrent applicants and 20 concurrent staff; it remains unverified under real load.

## Applicant flow

1. Explore opportunities, eligibility and application guidance.
2. Sign in with Google. The verified email is read-only; give recruitment processing consent before creating a draft.
3. Complete the profile, select one eligible opportunity and provide available days and three short written answers (motivation/contribution, collaboration and the role scenario), each nonblank and at most 150 words. New submissions require a view-only HTTPS resume link; portfolio links remain optional.
4. Save incomplete work and return later. Drafts remain editable outside intake and during pauses. Save errors/conflicts preserve local answers and offer explicit recovery.
5. Review and submit the acknowledged draft revision while intake accepts submissions. Submission creates one reference, immutable submitted answers and public history.
6. View status/history in the portal or withdraw. Withdrawal is final, retains the reference/history and prevents another submission.

**Eligibility:** Core Team accepts enrolled first- and second-year undergraduates. Committee accepts enrolled students across university year and program levels. Both tracks accept students from any university. Preserve the approved catalog, questions, field limits and capacities; [catalog.ts](../lib/catalog.ts) is their code source. Core Team has six vacancies; Committee has ten opportunities, including SkillBuilders and BuildHer+.

Each applicant can submit **one application across all sixteen opportunities**. Current and former staff use a separate Google account if they apply. Ordinary staff suspension/revocation does not convert a staff account into an applicant.

## Evaluation and decisions

- An administrator assigns one active evaluator; the application becomes **Under Review**. Initial assignment may include a reason; switching evaluators requires a private reason.
- The evaluator explicitly saves **Accepted**, **Waitlisted** or **Not Selected**, with optional private notes up to 5,000 Unicode code points. There is no numerical scoring or second simultaneous evaluator.
- Reassignment starts a fresh generation, including a return to an earlier evaluator. Only the current generation's saved review satisfies publication. The new evaluator can read each earlier assignment's latest saved review; full revisions and assignment reasons remain administrator-only.
- An administrator independently chooses, confirms and publishes an outcome. There is no saved-proposal stage. A saved current recommendation is required and remains valid if its evaluator later loses staff access. Publication is independent of intake.
- Acceptance cannot exceed capacity. Publication freezes reviews/assignments. Active current evaluators retain read-only published access; withdrawal removes their access.
- Amendments require a private reason, preserve history and recheck capacity without reopening evaluation. Accepted withdrawal or a change away from Accepted releases a place; nobody is promoted automatically.

Public statuses are **Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Waitlisted, Not Selected and Withdrawn**. Applicants never receive evaluator identities, recommendations, notes, private reasons or administrative audit data.

## Administrator tools

### Staff management

Staff sign in with Google and hold one administrator or evaluator role. Administrator access requires authenticator-app MFA. Every active MFA-verified administrator can grant existing verified accounts access, change roles, suspend/reactivate, revoke and explicitly restore access. Changes require a reason, confirmation and private history. Ordinary application commands block self-changes and removal of the last active administrator.

The project owner handles first-admin bootstrap and lost-authenticator recovery outside the app. QR enrollment has a manual-key fallback; displaying a QR or enrolling a factor alone never grants access. See [owner setup and recovery](architecture.md#owner-setup-and-recovery).

### Queue and export

Administrators search submitted applications by name/email/reference and filter status, track, opportunity, assignment and current-review state. Apply/Clear explicitly reset pagination. Counts follow every applied filter across all matching pages; Withdrawn is included by default and drafts are excluded.

CSV exports use **applied** filters across all matching pages. Clear filters to export all submitted applications. A fixed layout includes applicant/profile data, version-appropriate written answers, public state/times, consent choices and form version. Evidence links, drafts and private review/audit data are excluded. Files are generated on demand, bounded to 64 MiB and not retained by the app. See the [CSV contract](architecture.md#csv-export-contract).

### Intake

Administrators can pause, resume or **permanently close** intake with a private reason. Resume respects the fixed opening/deadline; after the deadline only Close remains. Close cannot be reversed through the app. Controls do not change dates/finalization or prevent draft saving, evaluation, decisions or withdrawal.
